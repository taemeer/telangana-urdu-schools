# Data audit and corrections

The government report contains 547 student records representing 178 source school-name entries. The published directory contains 177 distinct schools after these confirmed corrections:

1. `Z P H SCHOOL MUDHOLE` and `Z P HIGH SCHOOL,MUDHOLE` were merged as one school under `Z P H SCHOOL MUDHOLE`.
2. `Z P HIGH SCHOOL (EM), HASGUL` was corrected to `Z P HIGH SCHOOL (UM), HASGUL`.
3. The approved Urdu spelling `ہنوماکنڈہ` is used throughout.

Final totals: 31 districts, 113 mandals, 177 distinct schools, 71 Government schools, 106 Zilla Parishad schools and 36 girls' schools.
